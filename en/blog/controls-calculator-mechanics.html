Controls & Calculator Guide: Mastering the Cash Drawer & Shelf

Survival during your midnight shift depends heavily on quick service and sharp attention. Master every mouse interaction, learn how to hold right-click to check prices, and calculate Peso change without making costly errors.

Written by: Narinig Mo Ba Community • Published: September 9, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read
Quick Answer

In Narinig Mo Ba?, controls are centered on intuitive mouse interactions: Left Click to pick up or hand over goods, Hold Right Click over any shelf product to inspect its official price tag, and Hold Left Click to return misclicked items to the shelf. Giving change is done by clicking the desk's solar calculator to compute subtraction, then selecting coins (₱1, ₱5, ₱10, ₱20) and bills (₱50 to ₱1,000) inside the cash drawer.

The wooden cash drawer and solar calculator: Your primary tools for counting customer change.

1. Complete Mouse Control Reference Table

The controls designed by VC Studios are streamlined yet tactile. Here is the full breakdown of inputs and behaviors:

Input Action In-Game Effect Context / Usage
Edge Screen Panning Shifts camera perspective Between the shelf view and counter window
Single Left Click Grab or place merchandise On hanging sachets, drinks, or counter space
Hold Right Click Displays product price tag While hovering over any store item
Hold Left Click Safely returns item to shelf When an incorrect product was grabbed
Click Cash Drawer / Coins Opens drawer & counts money Located directly beneath the wooden counter

2. How to Inspect Product Prices (Hold Right Click)

A common pitfall for new players is guessing product costs and undercharging or overcharging customers.

Real-world sari-sari stores feature dozens of hanging single-use sachets (coffee, 3-in-1 mixes, shampoo, garlic chips, sardines). To prevent confusion, you can inspect any item at any time:

  • Hover your mouse directly over the item on the shelf or in your hand.
  • Press and hold Right Click.
  • An in-game indicator will display the item name and official price in Philippine Peso (₱).
  • Release the right-click button once confirmed.

3. Cash Drawer Currency System

The wooden drawer mechanism accurately reflects Filipino cash management:

🪙 Coins (Barya)

Used for small purchases such as candies or instant coffee:

₱1 Coin ₱5 Coin ₱10 Coin ₱20 Coin

💵 Banknotes (Papel)

Used when customers pay with large bills:

₱50 ₱100 ₱500 ₱1,000
⚡ Cashier Pro Tip: Always double-check your subtraction with the solar calculator on the counter before handing over coins. Giving the exact change on the first try keeps customer temperament calm during tense night encounters.
