Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

At Narinig Mo Ba? (accessible from https://narinigmoba.github.io), the privacy of our visitors is of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and record, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, passwords, credit card numbers, or sensitive personal data to play or explore our site. Standard web server logs collect anonymous traffic data, such as browser type, referring pages, timestamps, and general geographic region.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website utilizes cookies and HTML5 Local Storage for essential site functions:

  • Local Storage: Remembers your in-game progress, liked status, and locally posted comments on your device.
  • Consent Cookie: Stores your cookie consent decision (under nmb_consent).
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4: G-43EZV53Q7P) with IP masking to analyze aggregate traffic patterns.

Google AdSense and Advertising Partners

We may feature third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense and its advertising network. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to this and other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), visitors have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data. Since we do not maintain personal accounts, any cached information is stored locally on your device and can be cleared via your browser cache.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Contact Page.
